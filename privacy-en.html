Privacy Policy

Easy Video Downloader
Operator: gomeng-dev · Effective: October 7, 2026

This policy explains how the Android app Easy Video Downloader (io.gomeng.easydownloader) and this information website handle information.

Browsing and download records are kept on your device. The operator does not run a server to collect those records. Advertising, payments, websites you visit and storage you choose involve the processing described below.

The app does not automatically send browsing or download history. If you choose to submit the external report form described below, the operator receives your submitted content to investigate the problem and handle your inquiry.

1. Information processed on your device

  • Browser information: browsing records, visits resulting from searches, bookmarks, open tabs, cookies and website data, used for browsing, restoring tabs and applying your settings.
  • Downloads and media: media and source URLs, filenames, storage locations, download records, thumbnails, duration, size and other metadata, used for detection, downloads, playback, file management and duplicate download checks.
  • Settings and access: storage settings, remaining downloads, premium purchase status and advertising consent status needed to provide app features.
  • Custom storage: connection details you enter, including the network storage address, username, storage path and password, used to connect to your chosen storage.
  • Locks and clipboard: verification values for your PIN or password are stored on the device. Biometric authentication uses the Android authentication result; the app does not receive raw biometric information. If clipboard URL checking is enabled, the app checks for a URL while you use the app and asks whether to open it.

The app does not upload browsing URLs, media URLs, filenames or network storage credentials to an operator server or add them to advertising requests. It has no app account registration or browsing/download analytics service reporting to the developer.

2. Websites, search and external storage

Browsing, searches, thumbnails, media requests and downloads may send your IP address, user agent, request contents and necessary cookies or credentials to the website, search engine or media provider. Updating ad-blocking lists connects to the list provider. Those services also handle information under their own policies.

Your FTP/FTPS storage receives the authentication information needed for connection and the files and paths you choose. Opening, sharing or exporting through another app or storage location sends the information needed for that action to the destination you select.

3. Advertising and consent choices

The app uses Google AdMob rewarded ads to provide free download credits and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent management. The advertising SDK collects and shares the following for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it
  • App and advertising interactions, such as app launches, ad impressions, taps and video views
  • App and SDK performance and diagnostic information
  • Android advertising ID, app set ID and applicable device/account identifiers

Google's advertising services and advertising partners listed in the consent interface process information according to applicable settings and consent status. The app does not add your browsing or download lists to ad requests. Consent management is presented where required, and an advertising privacy choice in app settings lets you update your choice when applicable. You can manage your advertising ID in Android settings.

Further information about processing and retention: Google Privacy Policy, Google advertising technologies, AdMob SDK data disclosure.

4. Payments

Premium purchases use Google Play Billing. The app processes product details, purchase status and purchase tokens needed for confirmation and restoration, and keeps access status on your device. The operator does not directly receive card numbers or payment account information from the app. Google Play's policies and applicable law govern payment and transaction information.

5. Permissions and security

The app uses the Play Integrity API and a Cloudflare Workers verification server for app security and abuse prevention. Before a new media download starts, Google Play Store processes app metadata (package, version and signing certificate), Play app license status, device attestation information and the request hash. The hash uses the app package and a random identifier generated for each download attempt. It contains no browsing or media URLs, filenames, cookies, storage credentials, advertising ID or purchase token.

The app sends the encrypted token and random request identifier to the verification server over HTTPS. The server asks Google to decode the token, checks request binding, issuance time, app/device integrity and the Play installation license, then returns only an allow or deny result. This license check does not verify premium purchases. If verification fails or the server is unavailable, a new download does not start, its download credit is refunded and you can retry. Playing and managing media already kept on your device does not require this check.

The operator does not retain tokens, decoded verdicts or random request identifiers in logs or a database. Cloudflare processes IP addresses and connection information to deliver and secure the service. The server uses short-period IP-based request counters to limit excessive requests. Browsing and download records are not transmitted. Google and Cloudflare process information under their respective policies. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

Media and folder permissions display and save the files you allow. Notifications report download status. Network status supports options such as Wi-Fi downloads, and vibration is used for media detection and completion notices according to your settings.

Network storage passwords are encrypted on the device using an Android Keystore key. PIN/password verification values are kept separately from the original secret. App and item locks control access through the app; they do not encrypt media files themselves. Android automatic backup and device transfer are disabled for the app.

HTTP websites and FTP storage may transmit information without encryption. Use HTTPS and FTPS where supported. Device, external storage and destination access controls and security settings also affect data protection.

6. Retention, deletion and choices

Information on your device is kept while needed for app features or until you delete it. You can change browsing/download history preferences and delete records, cookies and website data in settings. Turning off download history may leave task and media information needed for file management or active downloads. Incognito tabs are excluded from ordinary browsing history and tab restoration, but files you explicitly save from an incognito tab remain at their destination.

You can delete saved files through the app. Uninstalling removes internal app data according to Android's uninstall behavior. Files in the media library, selected folders or network storage may remain and need to be deleted at that location. Use each external service's own methods to delete information retained by websites, Google or other services.

7. Information website and contact

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no additional ads, analytics scripts, input forms or tracking cookies. GitHub may process connection information, including visitor IP addresses, to provide and secure its service. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Privacy contact: public GitHub contact page. Submitting a request through your GitHub account makes your account name and message public. The operator reviews and uses them as needed to handle requests and maintain a response history; request records are kept on GitHub. You can use GitHub features and the contact page to edit or delete your own content or request deletion of a request record. GitHub's policies apply to the account and connection information it processes.

Do not post personal information, browsing/download records, video URLs or network storage passwords in a public request. General app or privacy questions can be submitted without this information.

The download problem button opens the Google report form in an external browser. The app does not automatically attach or transmit URLs, errors, country, files, identifiers or diagnostics. The page URL and issue type you submit, and any optional error or reproduction details, country, device/app version, suggestions and contact email you enter, are stored in Google Forms. The operator processes them only while needed to investigate issues, reply and improve app features, and deletes them when no longer needed. Report content and response summaries are not shown to other respondents. Sign-in and automatic email collection are not required. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

For deletion requests or email inquiries, contact gomeng-dev@protonmail.com. Email addresses and messages you send are also processed only while needed to resolve the inquiry and deleted when no longer needed. Your email provider's policies also apply. Do not include passwords, cookies, authentication tokens or unnecessary personal information in a form or email.

8. Changes

If app features or information handling change, this page and its effective date will be updated. Changes requiring additional notice or consent will be handled under applicable law and platform requirements.
